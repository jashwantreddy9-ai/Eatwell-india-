What is a balanced diet?

A balanced diet includes a variety of foods in suitable amounts to help the body get the energy and nutrients it needs. No single food provides everything.

🍚

Carbohydrates

Provide energy. Examples: rice, roti, millets, oats and potatoes.

🥚

Protein

Helps the body grow and repair. Examples: dal, beans, eggs, milk, curd, paneer and nuts.

🥕

Vitamins & minerals

Support many body functions. Eat different colourful vegetables and fruits.

🥜

Healthy fats

Needed by the body. Sources include nuts, seeds and suitable cooking oils.

💧

Water

Drink water regularly throughout the day. Needs vary with weather and activity.

🥬

Fibre

Supports digestion. Find it in vegetables, fruits, pulses and whole grains.

Build a balanced plate

A useful general guide is to include different food groups across your meals. Your needs may differ depending on age, activity, health and culture.

🥦 Vegetables & fruit

Include a variety of vegetables and seasonal fruits.

🌾 Grains & millets

Choose rice, roti, ragi, jowar or other grains.

🫘 Protein foods

Add dal, chana, beans, eggs, dairy, fish or other suitable options.

Simple Indian meal ideas

These are examples, not a strict diet plan. Adjust portions and choices to your appetite, needs and family food habits.

Breakfast

Idli or dosa with sambar, plus fruit or milk/curd.

Lunch

Rice or roti, dal, vegetables and curd.

Snack

Seasonal fruit, roasted chana, nuts or yoghurt.

Dinner

Roti or rice with dal/other protein and vegetables.

Remember: Healthy eating is about variety, balance and consistency — not skipping meals or following extreme food rules. For personal nutrition advice, speak with a qualified healthcare professional or dietitian.
